# Straion 💚 🌍

At Straion, we are committed to playing our part in the global efforts to combat climate
change and promote equality. We believe in the power of sustainable practices and are
cultivating a culture of environmental responsibility. Together, we can create a more
sustainable world.

## Remote Work and Optimized Development Cycles

Straion supports remote collaboration, significantly reducing the need for travel and helping
cut transportation-related CO2 emissions. Studies have shown that remote work can
substantially lower carbon footprints, particularly in the context of reduced commuting and
travel for global teams [1, 2]. By enabling a distributed workforce, we help minimize the
environmental impact of transportation, making a positive contribution to climate action.

We streamline development processes by improving decision clarity and minimizing delays or
errors. Shorter project timelines mean lower energy consumption throughout the software
development lifecycle, reducing the overall environmental impact. By continuously refining our
workflows, we contribute to [SDG 12](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal12) – Responsible Consumption and Production, particularly [target 12.2](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal12#targets_and_indicators), which aims to achieve sustainable management and efficient use of resources.

## Cloud Efficiency

Straion's infrastructure is hosted on AWS, a cloud platform that runs highly efficient data
centers and is investing heavily in renewable energy. Running on shared, optimized cloud
infrastructure lowers our energy use and carbon footprint compared to self-managed servers. This
supports [SDG 7](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal7) and its [target 7.2](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal7#targets_and_indicators), which calls for a substantial increase in the share of renewable energy in the global energy
mix by 2030. Learn more about [our provider's sustainability efforts here.](https://sustainability.aboutamazon.com/products-services/aws-cloud)

## Promoting Gender Equality

Straion champions inclusivity and equal opportunity. We ensure that all users have a voice in
the software decision-making process, supporting [SDG 5 – Gender Equality](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal5) and the target 5.1 – End all forms of discrimination against all women and girls everywhere.
We believe in empowering women by promoting their full and effective participation in leadership
at every level, from strategic development to project management. Our sustainability efforts reflect
our belief in balancing innovation with environmental and social responsibility, working towards
a better future for everyone.
